# Manzuri — Design Constitution

**Product in one line:** A micro social-media agency uploads a
client's posts once. The client gets one link — sent in the WhatsApp
chat they already use — taps Approve or Request changes, and every
decision is timestamped and locked to the version they saw. No login.
No "which screenshot was final?" archaeology.

**Identity: "Daylight agency desk."** Bright, warm white; ink text;
one green — the green of a sent WhatsApp message — used only for
things that are approved or sent. Where BolNote is a night studio
and Train Buffer is printed paper, Manzuri is a clean desk at 10 am:
the tool a two-person agency opens with their chai.

## Visual grammar
- Ground: warm white `#faf8f4`; panels `#ffffff`; ink `#20242a`;
  hairlines `#e5e0d5`.
- Accent: deep WhatsApp green `#0e7a5f` — approved states, the
  client link, primary actions. Signal red `#c8372d` only for
  "changes requested".
- Type: "Fraunces" display, "Inter" UI. Client page is phone-first:
  big tap targets, one post per screenful, buttons a shop owner can
  hit with a thumb while standing in their own shop.
- No dashboards full of charts. The agency home is the month wall
  (3D) + three counts that matter: waiting, approved, changes asked.

## The two surfaces (deliberately different)
- **Agency console** (desktop-first): create a client, add the
  month's posts, copy the client link, watch decisions land, export
  the approved-only checklist. The 3D month wall lives here.
- **Client page** (phone-first, no 3D, no login): the link IS the
  credential — a long unguessable token. Posts in date order, each
  with Approve / Request changes. A change request asks one
  structured question: is it the image, the caption, or both — plus
  a line of text if they want.

## The rule the product exists for
**Editing a post voids its approval.** If the agency changes the
caption or the creative after a client approved it, the approval is
gone — visibly, with the history kept. The approved version is the
version that gets exported. This one rule kills the wrong-version
dispute, which is the actual disease; everything else is plumbing.

## Voice
Plain words a Kirana-store owner and a 23-year-old agency runner
both understand. No "streamline your workflow synergy". The client
page speaks like a polite message from their agency, because that
is what it replaces.
